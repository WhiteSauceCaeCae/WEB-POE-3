About Sweet Lips Bakery

A local Cape Town favourite for cupcakes, cakes and artisanal treats.

Our Story

Sweet Lips Bakery is a local favourite cupcake and cake shop in Cape Town, offering a wide range of delicious and flavourful treats perfect for every occasion. Freshly baked to order with the best quality ingredients, our cakes and cupcakes promise an unforgettable taste in every bite or occasion.

Our Mission

At Sweet Lips Bakery, we are on a mission to make your experience as delightful as our sweet bakes. We believe in the power of teamwork to deliver quality baked goods and goods that surpass the expectations of our customers.

Our Promise

Discover the Sweet Lips difference, where great cakes, great service and the spirit of togetherness converge to create sweetness for every bite, big or small. Order online and enjoy convenient delivery of your favourite baked goods straight to your door.

Our Goals

  • Build a strong brand presence across Greater Cape Town.
  • Showcase an organised digital menu with daily offerings and dietary options.
  • Generate qualified leads through an interactive custom order form.
  • Drive foot traffic through location maps and operating hours.
